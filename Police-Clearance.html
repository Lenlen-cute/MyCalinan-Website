Police Clearance

Guide for requirements, steps, and online application.

Apply Online

You can apply for Police Clearance online using the official PNP system.

Apply Now

Requirements

  • Valid ID
  • Barangay Clearance
  • Recent 2x2 picture
  • Processing fee

Steps

  1. Register online or prepare requirements.
  2. Visit the police station.
  3. Submit documents and biometrics.
  4. Pay the fee.
  5. Wait for release.

Reminder

Always bring original IDs. Schedule online to avoid long lines.
