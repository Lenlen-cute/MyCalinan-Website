Calinan Poblacion

Calinan Poblacion, located in the 3rd District of Davao City and approximately 27 kilometers northwest of the city center, serves as the central barangay of Calinan District. Known as the "Fruit Basket of Davao City", it is famous for its abundant production of durian, bananas, and other tropical fruits. Originally inhabited by the Bagobo tribe under the leadership of "Datu Abeng", the area began as a small settlement surrounded by forests and clear streams. In the early 1900s, migrants from various parts of the Philippines settled in Calinan, contributing to its growth and cultural diversity. The development of roads, schools, and marketplaces transformed the community into a thriving agricultural and commercial center. Despite facing challenges such as war and devastating fires, the people of Calinan remained resilient and continued to rebuild and progress. Today, Calinan Poblacion stands as a vibrant and progressive barangay, playing a significant role in the trade, education, and agricultural development of Davao City.

Built for community growth, designed for Calinanians

MyCalinan is a web-based Smart Tourism and Community Information System designed to promote Calinan's tourist attractions, local businesses, cultural heritage, public services, and community activities through a centralized digital platform. Developed for residents, visitors, and barangay officials it provides easy access to tourism information, government services, community announcements, and barangay document inquiries. The system also features real-time location tracking, interactive navigation, live weather updates, and an AI-powered virtual assistant to enhance user experience. By bringing essential information and services into one platform, MyCalinan aims to support local development, strengthen community engagement, and improve accessibility within Calinan Poblacion.

Calinan

A peaceful and growing district of Davao City, is a place where nature and people live in harmony. It is known for its rich farmland, fresh fruits, and beautiful green surroundings that show the beauty of simple life. The community here is strong, kind, and always ready to help one another, making Calinan feel like a true home for everyone. Life may be simple, but it is full of meaning, hope, and hard work. Every sunrise brings new chances, and every day is a reminder that progress comes with unity and faith in each other. As the philosopher Lao Tzu once said, "The journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step." This reminds every Filipino that even small efforts can lead to great success. Calinan stands as a symbol of patience, growth, and the strength of Filipino spirit, inspiring everyone to keep moving forward in life.
